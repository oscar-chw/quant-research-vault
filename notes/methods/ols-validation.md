title: Linear-model fit state and cost-aware validation

A linear model estimates coefficients from a specified design matrix and target. Record the intercept choice, feature units, target horizon, training rows, fitted coefficients and any preprocessing. Winsorisation or scaling learned from training data must not be silently refitted on held-out outcomes.

Check a known numerical relation and adverse inputs before using market data. Then separate fitting, hyperparameter selection and later evaluation. Include costs, turnover and the meaning of fills; a signal correlation is not the same quantity as executable net performance.

Report uncertainty, a simple comparator and negative results. Sampler convergence, a passing implementation test or a more complex model does not prove economic edge. The cited HAR model is a particular volatility regression example, not a general profitable strategy or a risk-neutral pricing model.
